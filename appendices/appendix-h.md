# Appendix H: Seasonal Planting Guide and Academic Integration Calendar

## Overview

This guide aligns garden planting activities with the school academic calendar and the 5Rs framework. Planting decisions are made based on growing zone (Southeast U.S., Zone 7-8 as primary reference), academic standards alignment, and therapeutic value.

## Fall Semester (August - December)

### August - September: Establishing Foundations (Root Phase Emphasis)

**Garden Activities**:
- Clear and prepare beds from summer
- Soil testing (pH, nutrients, composition)
- Plant fall crops: lettuce, kale, broccoli, radishes, carrots, snap peas
- Establish herb garden (perennial herbs: rosemary, thyme, sage)
- Begin compost systems

**Academic Integration**:

| Subject | Activity | Standards Connection |
|---------|----------|---------------------|
| **Math** | Measure and map garden beds; calculate area and perimeter | Geometry, measurement |
| **Science** | Soil testing and analysis; weather data collection begins | Earth science, scientific method |
| **ELA** | Garden journal setup; observation writing; vocabulary development | Informative writing, domain vocabulary |
| **Social Studies** | Research cultural significance of fall crops; food systems | Cultural geography, economics |
| **SEL** | Regulation assessment baseline; grounding routine establishment | Self-awareness, self-management |

**5Rs Focus**: Establishing Root phase routines; introducing garden-based grounding activities.

### October - November: Active Growing and Learning (Full 5Rs Cycle)

**Garden Activities**:
- Monitor fall crop growth (daily observations)
- Thin seedlings; practice spacing calculations
- Plant garlic for spring harvest
- Harvest early crops (radishes, lettuce)
- Prepare cold frames for season extension
- Begin indoor seed starting (windowsill herbs)

**Academic Integration**:

| Subject | Activity | Standards Connection |
|---------|----------|---------------------|
| **Math** | Growth rate calculations; graphing data; fractions of harvest | Ratios, data analysis, fractions |
| **Science** | Photosynthesis investigation; life cycles; ecosystem observation | Life science, ecology |
| **ELA** | Procedural writing (planting guides); persuasive writing (garden proposals) | Informative and argument writing |
| **Social Studies** | Harvest traditions across cultures; food preservation history | Cultural studies, history |
| **SEL** | Full TRACE protocol implementation; regulation strategy building | All CASEL competencies |

### December: Reflection and Planning (Reflect/Reconnect Emphasis)

**Garden Activities**:
- Harvest remaining fall crops
- Mulch beds for winter protection
- Clean and store tools
- Indoor activities: seed catalog review, garden design planning

**Academic Integration**:

| Subject | Activity | Standards Connection |
|---------|----------|---------------------|
| **Math** | Yield calculations; cost-benefit analysis of garden season | Operations, data analysis |
| **Science** | Plant dormancy investigation; composting science | Life science, chemistry |
| **ELA** | Reflective writing; semester portfolio compilation | Narrative writing, self-reflection |
| **SEL** | Growth portfolio review; celebration of growth; goal setting | Self-awareness, responsible decision-making |

## Spring Semester (January - May)

### January - February: Indoor Cultivation (Reflect Phase Emphasis)

**Garden Activities**:
- Indoor seed starting under grow lights
- Germination experiments
- Garden design and planning for spring
- Repair and maintain garden infrastructure
- Composting maintenance

**Academic Integration**:

| Subject | Activity | Standards Connection |
|---------|----------|---------------------|
| **Math** | Seed spacing calculations; garden layout design (scale drawing) | Geometry, scale, measurement |
| **Science** | Germination variables experiment; seed anatomy; soil science | Scientific method, life science |
| **ELA** | Research reports on spring crops; garden design proposals | Research writing, informative text |
| **SEL** | Deep TRACE protocol work; reasoning move development | Metacognition, self-regulated learning |

### March - April: Spring Planting (Full 5Rs Cycle at Peak)

**Garden Activities**:
- Transplant seedlings to garden beds
- Direct sow warm-season crops (beans, squash, cucumbers)
- Plant pollinator garden (native wildflowers)
- Intensive data collection and observation
- Pest management and problem-solving

**Academic Integration**:

| Subject | Activity | Standards Connection |
|---------|----------|---------------------|
| **Math** | All operations through garden context; statistics projects | Grade-level math standards |
| **Science** | Ecology investigations; pollination studies; water cycle | Life science, earth science |
| **ELA** | Argumentative writing (environmental topics); garden blog/newsletter | All writing standards |
| **Social Studies** | Food justice; community food systems; agricultural economics | Civics, economics, geography |
| **SEL** | Advanced reasoning moves; peer regulation support; community projects | All CASEL competencies |

### May: Harvest and Celebration (Reconnect Phase Emphasis)

**Garden Activities**:
- Harvest spring crops
- Community harvest celebration
- Garden tours for families and community
- Summer maintenance planning
- Seed saving for next year

**Academic Integration**:

| Subject | Activity | Standards Connection |
|---------|----------|---------------------|
| **Math** | Final yield analysis; season comparison; financial summary | Data analysis, statistics |
| **Science** | Ecosystem health assessment; biodiversity survey | Ecology, environmental science |
| **ELA** | Capstone presentations; portfolio finalization; reflection essays | Speaking, writing, self-reflection |
| **SEL** | Growth celebration; mentoring transitions; summer planning | Community connection, agency |

## Recommended Crops by Purpose

| Purpose | Crops | Therapeutic/Academic Value |
|---------|-------|--------------------------|
| **Fast growth (motivation)** | Radishes, lettuce, beans | Visible results in 3-4 weeks; builds self-efficacy |
| **Sensory regulation** | Lavender, mint, rosemary, lemon balm | Aromatic calming; tactile engagement |
| **Data collection** | Tomatoes, sunflowers, beans | Measurable growth; multiple data points |
| **Cultural connection** | Varies by community | Connects families; honors heritage |
| **Nutrition education** | Kale, carrots, herbs, tomatoes | Harvest-to-table connection; cooking integration |
| **Ecosystem learning** | Native wildflowers, pollinator plants | Biodiversity; ecological relationships |
| **Long-term commitment** | Fruit trees, berry bushes, perennials | Patience; legacy; multi-year data |

# Appendix B: Regulation Level Assessment Tool

## Overview

The Regulation Level Assessment is used during the Root phase of every learning session to determine a student's readiness for academic engagement. The tool uses a 0-100 scale with five tiers that correspond to specific instructional responses.

## Assessment Scale

| Level | Range | State | Observable Indicators | Instructional Response |
|-------|-------|-------|----------------------|----------------------|
| **5** | 81-100 | Optimal | Engaged eye contact, responsive to questions, relaxed posture, curious, voluntarily participates | Proceed directly to Reflect phase |
| **4** | 61-80 | Adequate | Present but slightly distracted, responds when prompted, mild restlessness | Brief grounding activity (2-3 min), then proceed to Reflect |
| **3** | 41-60 | Borderline | Unfocused, restless, flat affect, slow to respond, passive | Extended grounding (5-8 min); reassess before proceeding |
| **2** | 21-40 | Dysregulated | Agitated, withdrawn, avoidant, negative self-talk, physical tension | Transition to Regulate phase; co-regulation strategies |
| **1** | 0-20 | Crisis | Distressed, dissociated, unsafe behavior, unable to communicate | Escalation protocol; immediate safety measures |

## Grade Band Assessment Methods

### K-2: Picture Card Check-In

Provide students with visual cards showing different states:

| Card | Image | Regulation Level |
|------|-------|-----------------|
| Sunshine | Bright sun with smile | 81-100 (Optimal) |
| Partly cloudy | Sun with some clouds | 61-80 (Adequate) |
| Cloudy | Full cloud cover | 41-60 (Borderline) |
| Rainy | Rain clouds | 21-40 (Dysregulated) |
| Stormy | Lightning and dark clouds | 0-20 (Crisis) |

**Procedure**: "Good morning! Pick the weather card that matches how you're feeling inside right now." Follow up with: "Tell me about your weather today."

### 3-5: Regulation Thermometer

Students point to or color in a vertical thermometer with numbered zones:

- **Green zone** (61-100): "Ready to grow!"
- **Yellow zone** (41-60): "Warming up -- I might need a minute."
- **Red zone** (0-40): "I need help getting regulated."

**Procedure**: "Check your inner thermometer. Where are you right now? What might help you move toward green?"

### 6-8: Self-Assessment Scale

Students self-report a number on the 0-100 scale, supported by an anchor chart:

**Procedure**: "Take a moment to check in with yourself. Where would you put your regulation level right now, on a scale of 0 to 100?" Follow up: "What's one thing that might shift your number up?"

### 9-12: Self-Directed Check-In

Students independently assess and record their regulation level in their growth journal. The educator reviews entries and follows up as needed.

**Procedure**: Students have a standing routine of journaling their regulation level, one contributing factor, and one strategy they plan to use if needed.

## Educator Observation Checklist

When student self-report may be unreliable (younger students, masked dysregulation), use this observation guide:

### Indicators of Regulation (Level 4-5)
- [ ] Makes appropriate eye contact
- [ ] Body posture is relaxed and open
- [ ] Responds to greetings and questions
- [ ] Shows curiosity or interest in the garden/activity
- [ ] Interacts appropriately with peers
- [ ] Transitions smoothly from previous activity

### Indicators of Dysregulation -- Hyperarousal (Level 1-2)
- [ ] Rapid or pressured speech
- [ ] Difficulty sitting still; excessive movement
- [ ] Disproportionate reactions to minor events
- [ ] Clenched fists, tight jaw, or rigid posture
- [ ] Argumentative or defiant responses
- [ ] Difficulty following multi-step instructions

### Indicators of Dysregulation -- Hypoarousal (Level 1-2)
- [ ] Flat affect; minimal facial expression
- [ ] Slow or absent responses to questions
- [ ] Appearing zoned out or sleepy
- [ ] Minimal spontaneous movement
- [ ] Avoiding eye contact
- [ ] Apparent indifference to activities

### Indicators of Masked Dysregulation
- [ ] Excessive perfectionism or people-pleasing
- [ ] Somatic complaints (headaches, stomachaches)
- [ ] Over-compliance without genuine engagement
- [ ] Anxious laughter or forced cheerfulness
- [ ] Social withdrawal disguised as independence

## Transition Decision Tree

```
Student arrives
       │
       ▼
  Regulation Check
       │
       ├── Level 81-100 ──► Proceed to REFLECT
       │
       ├── Level 61-80 ──► Brief grounding (2-3 min) ──► REFLECT
       │
       ├── Level 41-60 ──► Extended grounding (5-8 min)
       │                         │
       │                    Reassess
       │                         │
       │                    ├── Above 60 ──► REFLECT
       │                    └── Below 60 ──► REGULATE
       │
       ├── Level 21-40 ──► REGULATE phase
       │                         │
       │                    Co-regulation
       │                         │
       │                    Return to ROOT
       │
       └── Level 0-20 ──► Crisis protocol
                           Notify support staff
```

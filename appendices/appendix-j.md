# Appendix J: Research Bibliography and Recommended Reading

## Purpose

This bibliography provides the research foundation underlying every component of the FG2G curriculum. It is organized by domain to support educators, administrators, and researchers in deepening their understanding of the evidence base. Each section includes foundational works, key studies, and recommended reading for further exploration.

## Domain 1: Adverse Childhood Experiences (ACEs) and Trauma

### Foundational Works

| Citation | Significance to FG2G |
|---|---|
| Felitti, V. J., et al. (1998). Relationship of childhood abuse and household dysfunction to many of the leading causes of death in adults. *American Journal of Preventive Medicine, 14*(4), 245-258. | Original ACE Study establishing dose-response relationship between childhood adversity and adult health outcomes |
| van der Kolk, B. A. (2014). *The Body Keeps the Score: Brain, Mind, and Body in the Healing of Trauma.* New York: Viking. | Comprehensive account of trauma's impact on the body and brain; foundational to FG2G's somatic and embodied approach |
| Perry, B. D., & Szalavitz, M. (2017). *The Boy Who Was Raised as a Dog* (3rd ed.). New York: Basic Books. | Case studies demonstrating developmental impact of trauma; informs FG2G's developmental approach |
| Substance Abuse and Mental Health Services Administration. (2014). *SAMHSA's Concept of Trauma and Guidance for a Trauma-Informed Approach.* HHS Publication No. (SMA) 14-4884. | Defines the "Four Rs" (Realize, Recognize, Respond, Resist re-traumatization); foundation for FG2G's trauma-informed framework |

### Key Studies

| Citation | Relevance |
|---|---|
| Bethell, C. D., et al. (2017). Adverse childhood experiences: Assessing the impact on health and school engagement. *Pediatrics, 141*(4). | Documents ACEs' impact on school engagement; supports FG2G's integrated approach |
| Burke Harris, N. (2018). *The Deepest Well: Healing the Long-Term Effects of Childhood Adversity.* New York: Houghton Mifflin Harcourt. | Accessible overview of ACEs science and treatment; informs educator training |
| Crouch, E., et al. (2019). Prevalence of adverse childhood experiences (ACEs) among US children. *Child Abuse & Neglect, 92*, 209-218. | Updated prevalence data used in FG2G needs assessment frameworks |

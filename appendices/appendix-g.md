# Appendix G: Garden Journal Templates

## Overview

Garden journals serve as dual-purpose documents in the FG2G curriculum: they function as science notebooks for data collection and academic reflection, and as growth journals for social-emotional processing and metacognitive development. This appendix provides templates for each grade band.

## Portfolio Selection Guide

Students select journal entries for their growth portfolio based on:

| Selection Criterion | What to Look For |
|--------------------|-----------------|
| **Academic growth** | An entry that shows understanding you didn't have before |
| **Reasoning development** | An entry where you used a reasoning move effectively |
| **Error learning** | An entry where an error led to deeper understanding |
| **Regulation growth** | An entry that shows improved self-regulation over time |
| **Community connection** | An entry that connects garden learning to the real world |
| **Personal significance** | An entry that meant something important to you |

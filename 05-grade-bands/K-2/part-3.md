# K-2 Lesson Part 3: Sunlight and Thinking: Inquiry, Reasoning, and Meaning-Making

## 5Rs Phase Alignment
- **Primary phase:** Reflect
- **Garden metaphor:** Sunlight and Thinking represents how growth happens over time and with care.
- **Facilitator cue:** Name the phase explicitly so students can track where they are in the learning cycle.

## Learning Objectives
By the end of this lesson segment, early elementary learners will be able to:
1. Explain how the **Reflect** phase supports both emotional readiness and academic learning.
2. Complete age-appropriate tasks using play-based routines, sensory language, and short movement cycles.
3. Connect garden experiences to literacy, science, and social-emotional learning goals.

## Lesson Narrative
This part of the curriculum centers on **Reflect**. Educators open with a short community ritual, introduce a focused garden task, and then transition students into evidence-based discussion or creation. The lesson pacing for K-2 prioritizes play-based routines, sensory language, and short movement cycles. Throughout the session, staff use trauma-informed language, predictable transitions, and relationship-based feedback so learners can practice confidence and agency.

## Student Activities
1. **Opening Circle (5-10 minutes):** Grounding prompt tied to weather, seasons, or plant observations.
2. **Garden Task (15-30 minutes):** Hands-on work connected to the phase theme (reflect).
3. **Academic Bridge (15-25 minutes):** Translate garden evidence into classroom products such as picture journals, drawing labels, and oral share-outs.
4. **Collaborative Share (10 minutes):** Students teach back one strategy that helped them learn.

## Reflection Questions
- What did you notice in the garden that helped you think differently today?
- When did you need support, and what helped you keep growing?
- How does this phase (Reflect) show up in your learning outside the garden?
- What seed will you carry into the next lesson?

## Evidence of Learning
- Observation notes tied to participation, communication, and persistence.
- Student artifact review for alignment to lesson objectives.
- Quick self-rating on regulation, confidence, and understanding.

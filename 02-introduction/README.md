# Introduction - FG2G Curriculum

## Section Overview

This section introduces the From Garden to Growth (FG2G) curriculum, explaining its origins, purpose, and the urgent educational need it addresses. The documents in this section answer the fundamental question: *Why does this curriculum exist, and why does it matter?*

## Contents

| Document | Description |

| [Theoretical Rationale](theoretical-rationale.md) | Overview of the converging research traditions that inform the curriculum's design |

## Key Themes

- The inseparability of emotional safety and academic learning
- Garden-based education as a bridge between healing and instruction
- The need for curricula designed specifically for students who have experienced adversity
- Moving from "What's wrong with you?" to "What happened to you?" in educational settings

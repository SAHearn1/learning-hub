# Chapter 14: Financial Literacy

## Budgeting and Cash Flow
Track income, fixed expenses, variable expenses, and savings goals using monthly and weekly planning.

## Banking and Payment Systems
Compare checking and savings accounts, understand fees, digital payments, and fraud protection basics.

## Saving and Emergency Funds
Use pay-yourself-first strategies, short-term versus long-term saving goals, and emergency fund planning.

## Credit Scores and Reports
Identify factors that influence credit scores, how to read credit reports, and how to dispute errors.

## Loans and Interest
Analyze simple and compound interest, APR, loan terms, and total repayment cost.

## Taxes and Paychecks
Interpret pay stubs, gross versus net pay, common tax withholdings, and filing basics for teens.

## Insurance and Risk Management
Explain risk pooling, premiums, deductibles, coverage limits, and policy comparison.

## Investing Fundamentals
Understand risk-return tradeoffs, diversification, index funds, and long-term investing principles.

## Consumer Rights and Fraud Prevention
Recognize scams, evaluate offers, read contracts, and apply safe digital financial habits.

## Career, Income, and Opportunity Cost
Connect education, skills, and career pathways to earnings, benefits, and opportunity cost decisions.

## Entrepreneurship and Small Business Finance
Build basic revenue, expense, pricing, and break-even models for school-based ventures.

## Giving, Community Wealth, and Financial Goals
Set values-based goals that include giving, mutual aid, and long-term financial wellbeing.

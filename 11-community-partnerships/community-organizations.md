# Community Organization Directory

## Overview

Successful FG2G implementation relies on a network of community organizations that provide expertise, resources, and connections beyond what any single school can offer. This directory categorizes the types of organizations most relevant to FG2G partnerships, describes the roles they play, and provides guidance for identifying and engaging local partners in each category.

> *"No garden exists in isolation — it is part of a watershed, an ecosystem, a neighborhood. FG2G schools thrive when they are embedded in a rich network of community organizations that share the commitment to growing healthy young people."*

## Organization Categories

### Category 1: Agricultural and Horticultural Organizations

These organizations provide the garden expertise and resources essential to FG2G's hands-on learning approach.

| Organization Type | Role in FG2G | Typical Contributions |
|---|---|---|
| Cooperative Extension Services | Primary horticultural expertise partner | Master gardener volunteers, soil testing, planting calendars, pest identification, seasonal workshops |
| Community Garden Networks | Model garden programs; shared resources | Garden design consultation, seed libraries, tool sharing, community plot access |
| Botanical Gardens | Educational programming; plant resources | Field trip programs, plant donations, educator workshops, curriculum resources |
| Farm Bureau / Local Farms | Agricultural expertise; career connections | Farm visits, mentorship, produce distribution partnerships, agricultural career exploration |
| Native Plant Societies | Ecological expertise; indigenous planting knowledge | Native plant identification, habitat garden design, pollinator programming |
| Seed Libraries / Seed Banks | Heritage seed access; biodiversity education | Seed donations, seed saving workshops, cultural seed heritage programs |

**How to Find Local Partners:**
- Contact your county's Cooperative Extension Service (every U.S. county has one)
- Search for community garden networks through the American Community Gardening Association
- Visit local farmers markets to connect with regional agricultural organizations

### Category 2: Mental Health and Wellness Organizations

These organizations support the trauma-informed foundation of FG2G.

| Organization Type | Role in FG2G | Typical Contributions |
|---|---|---|
| Community Mental Health Centers | Clinical consultation; crisis referral pathway | Educator training on trauma; student referral access; consultation on regulation approaches |
| Trauma-Informed Care Networks | Specialized trauma expertise | Training on ACEs, trauma-informed practices; program consultation; fidelity review |
| School-Based Health Centers | On-site wellness support | Co-location of mental health services; integrated student support |
| Counseling Training Programs | Graduate student practicum placement | Counseling interns providing individual/group support within FG2G framework |
| Substance Abuse Prevention Programs | Prevention programming alignment | Aligned programming for adolescents; family support resources |
| Domestic Violence / Child Advocacy Centers | Specialized trauma support | Training on recognizing and responding to abuse; referral pathways; family support |

**How to Find Local Partners:**
- Contact your state's Department of Mental Health for community agency listings
- Connect with the National Child Traumatic Stress Network (NCTSN) for local affiliates
- Reach out to school district student services departments for existing partnerships

| Organization Type | Role in FG2G | Typical Contributions |
|---|---|---|

| Science Centers / Museums | STEM enrichment; field experiences | Field trips aligned to garden science; traveling exhibits; educator resources |

| Environmental Education Centers | Outdoor learning expertise | Nature-based programming; environmental science connections; outdoor classroom design |
| After-School Programs | Extended learning time | Garden maintenance during after-school hours; supplementary FG2G programming |

|---|---|---|

| Farmers Markets | Market experience; economic education | Student market booth opportunities; farm-to-school connections; entrepreneurship mentoring |
| Food Policy Councils | Food justice education | Student advocacy projects; food system analysis resources; policy engagement opportunities |
| School Food Services | Cafeteria-garden integration | Use garden produce in school meals; nutrition education collaboration; menu planning |

## Building an Organization Network

### Network Development Steps

1. **Inventory existing relationships** — Document all current school-community partnerships
2. **Identify gaps** — Map needs against the six categories above; identify underrepresented areas
3. **Prioritize outreach** — Focus on 2-3 high-priority partnerships per year
4. **Assign relationship owners** — Designate a staff member as primary contact for each partner
5. **Develop shared goals** — Collaboratively define mutual objectives with each partner
6. **Formalize agreements** — Create MOUs for sustained partnerships (see Partnership Models)
7. **Maintain and grow** — Regular communication, appreciation, and partnership health assessment

### Network Coordination

| Role | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| FG2G Coordinator | Maintains partnership directory; coordinates cross-partner activities; reports to administration |
| Educator Liaisons | Serve as day-to-day contacts with specific partner organizations |
| Family Advisory Council | Recommends community partners; facilitates family-organization connections |
| Administrator | Approves MOUs; allocates resources; provides institutional support |
| Students (9-12) | Participate in community mapping; co-design partnership activities; serve as youth ambassadors |

## Evaluating Organization Partnerships

### Annual Partnership Review

For each active partnership, assess:

| Dimension | Questions to Consider |
|---|---|
| Impact on students | Has this partnership measurably benefited students? |
| Mutual benefit | Is the partner organization also benefiting from the relationship? |
| Alignment with 5Rs | Which phases does this partnership support? Are there gaps? |
| Sustainability | Can this partnership continue long-term? What would help? |
| Growth potential | How could this partnership deepen or expand? |
| Relationship quality | Is communication strong? Are commitments being met? |

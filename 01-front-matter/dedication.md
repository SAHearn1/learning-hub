# Dedication

---

*This curriculum is dedicated to three communities of quiet strength.*

---

**To the students who carry the weight of adverse childhood experiences** -- those who walk into classrooms each morning bearing burdens no child should have to hold. You are not your trauma. You are not your diagnosis. You are not your worst day. You are seeds of extraordinary possibility, and this curriculum exists because your resilience has already proven what the research only tries to measure. Every lesson in these pages was written with your voice in mind, your story at its center, and your healing as its highest purpose.

**To the educators who show up with open hearts** -- the teachers, counselors, paraprofessionals, and school staff who choose, every single day, to meet children where they are rather than where a curriculum guide says they should be. You are the ones who notice the quiet child in the back row, who kneel down to eye level before asking a hard question, who understand that a dysregulated child is not a disrespectful child. Your compassion is a form of courage, and this work would mean nothing without your hands to carry it forward.

**To the gardens that teach us all about patience, resilience, and growth** -- the soil that accepts every seed without judgment, the roots that push through concrete, the plants that bend in storms and stand again when the wind passes. The garden does not ask a seed to justify its need for water. It does not punish the seedling that grows slowly. It simply provides the conditions for life and trusts the process. May our classrooms learn to do the same.

---

*And to every community that has dared to believe that healing is not only possible but is, in fact, the point of education.*

---

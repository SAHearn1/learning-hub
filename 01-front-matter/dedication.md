# Dedication

This curriculum is dedicated to the children who have been told -- by circumstance, by systems, by the weight of experiences no child should carry -- that they are not ready to learn.

You are ready. You have always been ready.

We simply needed to build the right garden.

---

To the educators who see the whole child -- not just the behavior, not just the test score, but the person underneath -- this work is yours. You are the non-anxious presence, the steady soil, the patient gardener who knows that growth happens on its own timeline.

To the families who have entrusted their children to our schools and our gardens -- your courage and your hope are the roots of everything we do.

And to every student who has ever felt like a seed buried too deep to reach the light: this curriculum was built for you. The garden is waiting, and it does not care about your past. It only cares about what you are becoming.

---

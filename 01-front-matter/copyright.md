## Disclaimer

The information contained in this curriculum is provided for educational purposes. While the curriculum has been developed based on peer-reviewed research in trauma-informed pedagogy, therapeutic horticulture, and cognitive science, it is not a substitute for professional clinical intervention. Students exhibiting signs of acute trauma or mental health crisis should be referred to qualified mental health professionals.

The garden-based activities described in this curriculum should be conducted under appropriate adult supervision and in compliance with all applicable safety standards, allergy protocols, and local regulations.

## Research Citations

The theoretical foundations of this curriculum draw on published research from the following fields:

- Polyvagal Theory (Porges, 2011)
- Window of Tolerance framework (Siegel, 1999)
- Growth Mindset research (Dweck, 2006)
- Self-Regulated Learning (Zimmerman, 2002)
- Experiential Learning Theory (Kolb, 1984)
- Adverse Childhood Experiences studies (Felitti et al., 1998)
- Therapeutic Horticulture research (Simson & Straus, 1998; Sempik, Hine & Wilcox, 2010)

Full citations are provided in the Literature Review (Section 03) and Appendix J.

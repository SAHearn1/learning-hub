# Title Page

## Full Title
**From Garden to Growth: A Trauma-Informed, 5Rs-Aligned Curriculum for Academic, Social-Emotional, and Community Learning**

## Authorship and Sponsoring Organization
- **Lead author:** Dr. Shawn A. Hearn
- **Organization:** Community Exceptional Children's Services
- **Program model:** RootWork and the 5Rs Framework

## Intended Audience
This guide is designed for K-12 educators, instructional coaches, school counselors, administrators, community partners, and families implementing garden-based, trauma-informed learning.

## Edition Information
- **Edition:** Core Curriculum Manuscript Import Edition
- **Format:** Living curriculum (updated iteratively with implementation data)
- **Companion resources:** Grade-band lesson sets, assessment tools, implementation guide, appendices, and digital platform integration guidance.

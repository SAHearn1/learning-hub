# From Garden to Growth (FG2G)

## A Trauma-Informed, Garden-Based Curriculum for K-12 Education

### Integrating the 5Rs Framework, Therapeutic Horticulture, and the TRACE Protocol

---

**Author**: Dr. Shawn A. Hearn

**Organization**: Community Exceptional Children's Services

**Edition**: First Edition

**Year**: 2025

---

### Curriculum Components

| Component | Description |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Pedagogical Framework** | 5Rs: Root, Regulate, Reflect, Restore, Reconnect |
| **Cognitive Protocol** | TRACE: Think, Reason, Articulate, Check, Extend |
| **Learning Environment** | Living Learning Labs -- garden-based instructional spaces |
| **Technology Platform** | RootGuide AI Tutor with dysregulation detection |
| **Grade Coverage** | K-2, 3-5, 6-8, 9-12 |
| **Subject Integration** | Mathematics, Science, ELA, Social Studies, SEL |

---

### Standards Alignment

- Georgia Standards of Excellence (GSE)
- Common Core State Standards (CCSS)
- Next Generation Science Standards (NGSS)
- CASEL Social-Emotional Learning Framework

---

*This curriculum was developed at the intersection of trauma-informed pedagogy, therapeutic horticulture, and cognitive science to serve students who have experienced adverse childhood experiences (ACEs). Every element -- from the garden bed to the AI tutor -- is designed to create learning environments where healing and academic growth happen together.*

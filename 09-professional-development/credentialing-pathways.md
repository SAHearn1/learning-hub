# FG2G Credentialing Pathways

## Overview

The FG2G credentialing system recognizes educator growth through a structured progression that mirrors the curriculum's developmental approach. Just as students move through the 5Rs toward increasing independence and mastery, educators advance through credentialing tiers that reflect deepening expertise, broader impact, and growing leadership capacity.

> *"Credentialing in FG2G is not a gatekeeping exercise — it is a trellis, providing structure and support as educators grow toward their fullest professional expression."*

## Credentialing Tiers

### Tier 1: FG2G Foundation Certificate

**Purpose:** Validates completion of foundational training and readiness for supported implementation.

**Requirements:**

| Requirement | Description | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Training hours | Complete 40-hour Foundational Intensive + 20-hour Garden Practicum | Attendance records, facilitator verification |
| Knowledge assessment | Demonstrate understanding of core concepts | Written assessment (80% minimum) |
| Garden competency | Complete basic horticultural skills checklist | Skills demonstration, facilitator sign-off |
| Lesson design | Submit 3 dual-purpose lesson plans incorporating the 5Rs | Lesson plans reviewed by mentor |
| Reflection | Write a 1,000-word reflective narrative on personal connection to FG2G principles | Narrative evaluated against reflection rubric |

**Timeline:** Awarded upon completion of summer training institute
**Renewal:** Valid for 2 years; renewed by completing continuing education requirements

### Tier 2: FG2G Certified Practitioner

**Purpose:** Recognizes demonstrated competence in independent FG2G implementation with evidence of student impact.

**Prerequisites:** Tier 1 certificate + minimum 1 full year of implementation

**Requirements:**

| Requirement | Description | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Implementation fidelity | Demonstrate consistent implementation across all 5Rs phases | Fidelity rubric self-assessment + observer verification |
| Student impact data | Show measurable improvement in student outcomes | Pre/post regulation data, academic metrics, SEL measures |
| Teaching portfolio | Document teaching practice with annotated artifacts | 5 video recordings with written analysis; 10 lesson plans with student work samples |
| Garden program | Maintain a functional garden learning space | Photo documentation, seasonal plans, maintenance records |
| Professional learning | Complete all 6 PD modules + 20 additional hours | Training records, PLC participation logs |
| TRACE facilitation | Demonstrate fluent TRACE protocol facilitation | Video evidence with facilitator assessment |
| Peer collaboration | Participate actively in PLC and peer observation | PLC attendance records, peer observation reports |
| Action research | Conduct a classroom-based inquiry project | Written report (3,000-5,000 words) with data analysis |

**Timeline:** Earliest eligibility after 1 full year of implementation
**Renewal:** Valid for 3 years; renewed by submitting updated portfolio and continuing education credits

### Tier 3: FG2G Master Facilitator

**Purpose:** Recognizes expertise sufficient to train, mentor, and lead FG2G implementation for other educators and schools.

**Prerequisites:** Tier 2 certification + minimum 2 additional years of implementation

**Requirements:**

| Requirement | Description | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Advanced implementation | Demonstrate innovative, contextually adapted implementation | Portfolio of adapted lessons, curriculum modifications |
| Mentorship | Successfully mentor at least 2 Tier 1 educators through their first year | Mentee evaluations, mentor reflection logs |
| PD facilitation | Co-facilitate at least 3 PD sessions under supervision | Facilitator assessment, participant evaluations |
| Research contribution | Conduct and present action research or contribute to program evaluation | Published report, conference presentation, or program evaluation document |
| School-wide impact | Document influence beyond individual classroom | Evidence of school culture change, colleague adoption, administrative support |
| Garden leadership | Develop or expand garden programs beyond personal classroom | Documentation of garden program development |
| Advanced training | Complete Master Facilitator institute (40 additional hours) | Attendance records, facilitator verification |
| Professional network | Active participation in FG2G educator network | Network activity logs, collaborative projects |

**Timeline:** Earliest eligibility after 3 total years of implementation
**Renewal:** Valid for 5 years; renewed through continued mentorship and professional contribution

## Credentialing Process

### Application and Review

```
APPLICATION          PORTFOLIO REVIEW         INTERVIEW/DEMO         DECISION
────────────         ────────────────         ──────────────         ────────
Submit portfolio  →  Panel of 2 Master    →   30-min interview   →   Certificate
and application      Facilitators review      + teaching demo        awarded or
form                 evidence against         (Tiers 2-3 only)      revision plan
                     rubric criteria                                 provided
```

### Review Criteria

All portfolio evidence is evaluated using a 4-point scale:

| Score | Descriptor | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 4 | Distinguished | Exceeds expectations; demonstrates innovation and depth |
| 3 | Proficient | Meets all expectations with consistency and quality |
| 2 | Developing | Meets some expectations; specific areas need strengthening |
| 1 | Beginning | Does not yet meet expectations; substantial growth needed |

**Minimum scores for certification:**
- Tier 1: Average of 2.5 across all criteria
- Tier 2: Average of 3.0 across all criteria, no score below 2
- Tier 3: Average of 3.5 across all criteria, no score below 3

### Revision and Resubmission

If an application does not meet minimum requirements:
1. Candidate receives detailed written feedback identifying specific areas for growth
2. Candidate develops a growth plan with mentor support
3. Candidate may resubmit after a minimum of 3 months
4. Resubmission is reviewed by the same panel for consistency

## Continuing Education Requirements

### Annual Requirements by Tier

| Activity | Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| PD workshop hours | 20 hours | 16 hours | 12 hours |
| PLC participation | Monthly | Monthly | Monthly + facilitation |
| Garden maintenance | Document active garden | Document active garden | Document program expansion |
| Student data submission | Annual summary | Semester reports | Semester reports + analysis |
| Peer observation | 2 observations given/received | 4 observations given/received | 6 observations + mentorship |
| Professional reading | 2 articles/books | 3 articles/books | 3 articles/books + 1 shared |
| Conference participation | Attend 1 session | Attend or present | Present at 1+ conference |

### Approved Continuing Education Sources

- FG2G Professional Development workshops and institutes
- Community Exceptional Children's Services training events
- National conferences on trauma-informed education, therapeutic horticulture, or SEL
- University courses in related fields (education, horticulture, psychology, social work)
- Self-directed study with documentation and mentor approval
- Peer learning circle facilitation or participation
- Action research and program evaluation projects

## Institutional Partnerships

### University Credit Alignment

FG2G credentialing hours may be applied toward:
- Graduate certificates in trauma-informed education
- Master's degree coursework in special education, counseling, or curriculum and instruction
- Continuing education units (CEUs) for teaching license renewal
- American Horticultural Therapy Association (AHTA) professional registration hours

### School District Integration

Districts implementing FG2G may:
- Recognize FG2G credentials in salary advancement schedules
- Include FG2G competencies in teacher evaluation frameworks
- Apply FG2G PD hours toward state-required professional development
- Use FG2G credentialing as evidence for school improvement plans

## Quality Assurance

### Program Evaluation

The FG2G credentialing program is evaluated annually through:

1. **Candidate outcomes** — Certification rates, time to certification, revision rates
2. **Student impact** — Aggregated outcomes data from credentialed educators
3. **Candidate satisfaction** — Post-certification surveys and interviews
4. **Reviewer calibration** — Annual training to ensure consistent scoring
5. **External review** — Biennial review by advisory board including researchers, practitioners, and community members

### Ethical Standards

All FG2G credentialed educators commit to:
- Maintaining the safety and well-being of all students as the primary obligation
- Practicing within the scope of their training and credentialing level
- Seeking consultation when encountering situations beyond their competence
- Maintaining confidentiality regarding student regulation data and trauma histories
- Engaging in ongoing professional growth and self-reflection
- Supporting the growth and development of colleagues
- Representing the FG2G framework accurately and with fidelity

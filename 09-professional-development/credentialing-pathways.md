# Credentialing Pathways

## Overview

The RootWork credentialing system recognizes educators who demonstrate sustained commitment to trauma-informed, garden-based instruction through the 5Rs Framework. Three credential levels -- Practitioner, Specialist, and Master Educator -- correspond to the three-year professional development trajectory and represent increasing depth of knowledge, implementation skill, and leadership capacity.

These credentials are designed to:

- Validate educators' professional growth in trauma-informed, garden-based pedagogy
- Provide a clear progression pathway that sustains engagement beyond initial training
- Build a cadre of site-based leaders who can sustain and scale the RootWork model
- Connect to existing Georgia educator certification requirements and university graduate credit opportunities
- Signal to families, administrators, and communities that an educator has met rigorous standards of practice

## Credential Level 1: RootWork Practitioner

### Description

The RootWork Practitioner credential is awarded to educators who complete the Year 1 Foundation professional development program and demonstrate foundational competency in the 5Rs Framework, TRACE protocol, garden-based pedagogy, and trauma-informed classroom practices.

### Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| PD Hours | 40 hours (Year 1 Foundation program) |
| Session Attendance | All 8 core PD sessions completed |
| Online Modules | All Year 1 online modules completed with passing scores (80% or higher) |
| Coaching Cycle | Minimum 1 coaching cycle (pre-observation, observation, debrief) |
| Peer Observation | Minimum 1 peer observation completed with written reflection |
| Implementation Evidence | Minimum 4 documented 5Rs lesson cycles (1 per quarter) |
| Garden Activity | Minimum 4 garden-based learning activities documented |
| Self-Assessment | Completion of the Year 1 Self-Assessment across all 6 competency domains |
| Reflection Portfolio | Submission of a foundational portfolio (see portfolio requirements below) |

### Portfolio Requirements: Practitioner Level

The Practitioner portfolio is a curated collection of artifacts that demonstrates foundational competency. It includes:

1. **Professional Growth Narrative (2-3 pages):** A written reflection describing the educator's learning journey through Year 1, including shifts in beliefs, practices, and understanding of trauma-informed education.

2. **5Rs Lesson Plans (minimum 4):** Lesson plans demonstrating the 5Rs structure across at least two content areas. Each plan must include the educator's annotations about what worked, what was adjusted, and what they would change.

3. **TRACE Documentation (minimum 2 examples):** Transcripts, audio recordings, or detailed notes from TRACE facilitation showing the use of questioning stems and reasoning moves.

4. **Garden Activity Documentation (minimum 4 entries):** Photos, student work samples, and brief descriptions of garden-based learning activities with standards alignment noted.

5. **Regulation Practice Log:** Documentation of the educator's personal regulation practice across at least 8 weeks, including strategies used and reflections on effectiveness.

6. **Student Voice (minimum 2 samples):** Student reflections, quotes, or survey responses that speak to the impact of the 5Rs approach on their learning experience.

### Observation Requirements: Practitioner Level

- Minimum 1 formal coaching observation using the RootWork Observation Protocol
- Minimum 1 peer observation (observing a colleague) with written reflection
- The coaching observation must demonstrate presence of at least 4 of the 5 phases of the 5Rs in a single lesson
- Observation feedback is formative, not evaluative -- it supports growth rather than gatekeeping

## Credential Level 2: RootWork Specialist

### Description

The RootWork Specialist credential is awarded to educators who complete the Year 2 Advanced Practice program and demonstrate consistent, high-quality implementation of the RootWork model with measurable student impact.

### Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Prerequisite | RootWork Practitioner credential |
| PD Hours | 60 cumulative hours (40 Year 1 + 20 Year 2) |
| Advanced Sessions | All Year 2 advanced workshop sessions completed |
| Online Modules | All Year 2 advanced online modules completed |
| Coaching Cycles | Minimum 2 coaching cycles during Year 2 |
| Action Research | Completion of a site-based action research project |
| Implementation Evidence | Demonstrated consistent 5Rs implementation across the full school year |
| Student Data | Evidence of student progress in academic and/or SEL outcomes |
| Community of Practice | Active participation in a school-based professional learning community |
| Specialist Portfolio | Submission of an advanced portfolio (see requirements below) |

### Portfolio Requirements: Specialist Level

The Specialist portfolio builds on the Practitioner portfolio and adds:

1. **Implementation Narrative (3-5 pages):** An in-depth reflection on the second year of implementation, including how practice has deepened, challenges encountered, and adjustments made based on data.

2. **Original Lesson Plans (minimum 4):** Educator-designed (not adapted) lesson plans that demonstrate original integration of the 5Rs, TRACE, and garden-based learning. Must include differentiation for diverse learners.

3. **Action Research Report (5-8 pages):** A formal write-up of the educator's site-based action research project, including research question, methodology, findings, and implications for practice.

4. **Student Outcome Data:** Quantitative and/or qualitative evidence of student growth. This may include pre/post assessment data, portfolio growth analysis, regulation tracking data, or SEL survey results.

5. **Peer Feedback Evidence:** Feedback received from peer observations, including evidence of how the educator used feedback to adjust practice.

6. **RootGuide Data Analysis:** Screenshots or reports from the RootGuide platform demonstrating how the educator used data to inform instruction, with a written reflection on data-driven decision-making.

7. **Cultural Responsiveness Artifact:** Evidence of culturally responsive garden-based instruction, such as a lesson that incorporates diverse agricultural traditions, student cultural knowledge, or family engagement.

### Observation Requirements: Specialist Level

- Minimum 2 formal coaching observations during Year 2
- At least 1 observation must score "High Fidelity" on the RootWork Observation Protocol across all 5 domains (5Rs Facilitation, TRACE Implementation, Garden Integration, Regulation Practices, Student Engagement)
- Minimum 2 peer observations (observing colleagues) with written reflections
- Minimum 1 observation of a Year 1 educator with informal mentoring feedback provided

## Credential Level 3: RootWork Master Educator

### Description

The RootWork Master Educator credential is the highest level of recognition in the RootWork system. It is awarded to educators who demonstrate excellence in implementation, leadership in professional learning, and evidence of sustained positive impact on students, colleagues, and the school community.

### Requirements

| Requirement | Details |
|-------------|---------|
| Prerequisite | RootWork Specialist credential |
| PD Hours | 80 cumulative hours (40 + 20 + 20) |
| Leadership Institute | Completion of the Year 3 Leadership Institute (12 hours) |
| Mentorship Practicum | Mentoring at least 1 Year 1 educator through a complete coaching cycle (minimum 4 observations with pre/post conferences) |
| Facilitation | Co-facilitation of at least 1 PD session under the supervision of a trained facilitator |
| Professional Contribution | Presentation at a school, district, regional, or national conference OR publication in a professional journal or blog |
| Master Portfolio | Submission and defense of a comprehensive Master Educator portfolio |
| Portfolio Defense | Oral presentation and Q&A with a review panel (30-45 minutes) |

### Portfolio Requirements: Master Educator Level

The Master Educator portfolio is a comprehensive evidence collection that demonstrates mastery across all six competency domains and leadership capacity. It includes:

1. **Leadership Philosophy Statement (3-5 pages):** An articulation of the educator's philosophy of trauma-informed, garden-based education, including how they envision their role as a leader and mentor.

2. **Three-Year Growth Narrative (5-8 pages):** A reflective account of the educator's full professional development trajectory, including key turning points, challenges overcome, and sustained growth.

3. **Mentorship Documentation:** Evidence of effective mentoring, including observation notes, feedback provided, mentee reflections, and a summary of the mentee's growth.

4. **Facilitation Evidence:** Artifacts from PD facilitation, including session plans, participant feedback, and self-reflection on facilitation effectiveness.

5. **Longitudinal Student Outcome Data:** Student growth data spanning at least 2 years, demonstrating sustained positive impact on academic achievement, SEL development, and/or regulation skills.

6. **Action Research or Inquiry Project:** An extended inquiry project that contributes new knowledge to the field of trauma-informed, garden-based education.

7. **Professional Contribution Artifact:** The conference presentation, publication, or other professional contribution, with a reflection on its development and impact.

8. **Cultural Responsiveness Portfolio Section:** Extended evidence of culturally responsive practice, including community engagement, family partnership, and integration of diverse knowledge systems.

9. **Personal Regulation Practice Reflection:** A candid reflection on the educator's own regulation journey, including strategies that sustain them in the work and how they manage secondary traumatic stress.

### Portfolio Defense

The portfolio defense is a 30-45 minute oral presentation followed by questions from a review panel. The panel typically includes:

- A RootWork program director or designee
- A current RootWork Master Educator
- A school administrator or district representative
- An external reviewer (university partner, community organization representative)

The defense is not a test; it is a celebration of professional growth and a conversation about the educator's vision for continuing to lead trauma-informed, garden-based practice.

### Observation Requirements: Master Educator Level

- Minimum 2 formal coaching observations during Year 3, both scoring "High Fidelity" across all domains
- Minimum 1 observation of the educator facilitating a PD session, evaluated using the Facilitator Observation Protocol
- Minimum 4 observations conducted by the educator as a mentor, with documented feedback and mentee growth evidence

## Credential Renewal

All RootWork credentials require annual renewal to ensure that educators maintain active engagement with the model and continue their professional growth.

### Renewal Requirements

| Credential Level | Annual PD Hours | Additional Requirements |
|-----------------|----------------|------------------------|
| Practitioner | 20 hours | Continued implementation evidence; updated self-assessment |
| Specialist | 20 hours | Continued implementation evidence; participation in community of practice; 1 peer observation |
| Master Educator | 20 hours | Continued implementation; mentoring at least 1 educator; facilitation of at least 1 PD activity; updated portfolio artifact |

### Acceptable Renewal Activities

- Attending RootWork PD sessions or advanced workshops
- Completing online learning modules
- Participating in coaching cycles (as coach or educator)
- Conducting or participating in peer observations
- Presenting at professional conferences
- Publishing professional writing related to RootWork practices
- Completing university coursework in related fields
- Participating in garden training (Master Gardener courses, permaculture design)
- Attending approved external PD in trauma-informed practice, SEL, or culturally responsive teaching

### Lapsed Credentials

If an educator does not complete renewal requirements within the designated year, the credential enters a "lapsed" status. Educators have one additional year to complete renewal requirements and restore active status. After two consecutive years without renewal, the educator must complete a re-entry process:

- Practitioner: Complete 10 hours of refresher PD and submit an updated portfolio section
- Specialist: Complete 20 hours of refresher PD, 1 coaching cycle, and submit updated portfolio sections
- Master Educator: Complete 20 hours of refresher PD, 1 coaching cycle, 1 mentorship cycle, and present an updated portfolio defense

## Connection to Georgia Educator Certification

### Georgia Professional Standards Commission (GaPSC) Alignment

RootWork PD hours are designed to align with GaPSC requirements for certificate renewal:

- All RootWork PD hours qualify as PLU (Professional Learning Unit) credits when approved by the local school district
- The RootWork program provides documentation of hours, competencies addressed, and activities completed in a format compatible with GaPSC reporting requirements
- Educators pursuing certification in Special Education, ESOL, or Gifted may find that specific RootWork competencies (trauma science, cultural responsiveness, differentiation) align with endorsement requirements

### Certification Endorsement Pathway (In Development)

RootWork is actively pursuing recognition as a state-approved certification endorsement in Trauma-Informed, Garden-Based Education through the GaPSC. This endorsement would:

- Appear on the educator's Georgia teaching certificate
- Require completion of the full three-year RootWork PD trajectory (80 hours)
- Include a standardized assessment component
- Be renewable through ongoing PD and implementation evidence

The endorsement pathway is currently in the proposal phase. Updates will be communicated through the RootWork program office.

## University Partnership Pathway

### Graduate Credit Option

Through partnerships with accredited universities in Georgia, educators may earn graduate credit for RootWork professional development. The current partnership structure allows:

| PD Component | Graduate Credits | University Partner |
|--------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Year 1 Foundation (40 hours) | 3 semester hours | Partnership pending |
| Year 2 Advanced (20 hours) | 2 semester hours | Partnership pending |
| Year 3 Leadership (20 hours) | 2 semester hours | Partnership pending |
| Action Research Project | 1-3 semester hours | Partnership pending |
| Master Educator Portfolio | 1-3 semester hours | Partnership pending |

### Pathway to Advanced Degree

Educators who complete the full RootWork credentialing trajectory may apply up to 12 semester hours toward a graduate degree in education, curriculum and instruction, or a related field at partner institutions. This creates a pathway for educators to pursue a master's degree or specialist degree while simultaneously deepening their RootWork practice.

### University Research Partnership

Master Educator credential holders are encouraged to participate in university-based research partnerships, contributing to the evidence base for trauma-informed, garden-based education. Participation may include:

- Serving as a research site for graduate student studies
- Co-authoring publications with university faculty
- Presenting at university-hosted research symposia
- Participating in longitudinal outcome studies
- Contributing to curriculum development and validation research

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I start at a credential level higher than Practitioner?**
A: Educators with extensive prior training in trauma-informed practice or garden-based education may apply for an accelerated pathway review. If prior training substantially covers Year 1 competencies, an abbreviated entry may be approved. Contact the RootWork program office.

**Q: What if I change schools during the credentialing process?**
A: RootWork credentials are portable and belong to the individual educator, not the school. If you transfer to a new school, your credential status and PD hours transfer with you. The new school may need to provide implementation support.

**Q: Can paraprofessionals, counselors, or administrators earn RootWork credentials?**
A: Yes. The RootWork credentialing pathway is open to all school-based professionals who implement the 5Rs Framework. Modified portfolio requirements may apply for non-classroom roles.

**Q: How much does the credentialing process cost?**
A: There is no separate credentialing fee for educators participating in a RootWork partner school. Portfolio review and credential issuance are included in the program partnership. Educators pursuing graduate credit will pay university tuition at the partner institution's rate.

**Q: Is the RootWork credential recognized outside of Georgia?**
A: The RootWork credential is a program-specific credential currently recognized by RootWork partner schools and districts. As the program expands to additional states, reciprocity agreements will be established. The credential portfolio and PD hours are portable and can support certification renewal in most states.

# Video Library Index

## Overview

The RootWork Video Library is a curated collection of training videos designed to support professional development across all competency domains. Videos are hosted on the RootGuide platform and accessible to all credentialed educators. Each video includes closed captions, a downloadable transcript, and an accompanying reflection guide.

Videos are organized into eight categories aligned with the RootWork PD competency domains and implementation priorities. All videos are produced by the RootWork team in partnership with practicing educators and are updated annually to reflect current research and practice.

---

## Category 1: 5Rs Framework Demonstrations

### VL-1.1: Introduction to the 5Rs Framework
- **Duration:** 18 minutes
- **Description:** A comprehensive overview of the 5Rs Framework (Root, Regulate, Reflect, Restore, Reconnect), including theoretical foundations, the garden growth metaphor, and the responsive flow between phases. Narrated by the curriculum designer with animated diagrams and classroom footage.
- **Target Audience:** All educators (Year 1)
- **Learning Objectives:** Name and describe all five phases; explain the garden metaphor; distinguish between linear and responsive implementation.

### VL-1.2: The Root Phase in Action (K-2)
- **Duration:** 12 minutes
- **Description:** A filmed lesson segment showing a kindergarten teacher leading a Root phase grounding activity using garden sensory materials. Includes teacher narration explaining decision-making in real time.
- **Target Audience:** K-2 educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Observe age-appropriate grounding strategies; identify indicators of student readiness; note transition cues from Root to Regulate.

### VL-1.3: The Root Phase in Action (3-5)
- **Duration:** 14 minutes
- **Description:** A third-grade classroom opening where the teacher uses a brief garden observation journal entry as the Root phase. Students write and share observations before transitioning to the academic lesson.
- **Target Audience:** 3-5 educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Observe literacy-integrated grounding; see student-led sharing routines; note how Root connects to content.

### VL-1.4: The Root Phase in Action (6-8)
- **Duration:** 11 minutes
- **Description:** A middle school science class opening where students complete a 3-minute garden check-in protocol, recording observations of their assigned plant plots before beginning an ecosystem lesson.
- **Target Audience:** 6-8 educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Observe data-driven grounding activities; see student autonomy in Root phase; note adolescent engagement strategies.

### VL-1.5: The Root Phase in Action (9-12)
- **Duration:** 13 minutes
- **Description:** A high school environmental science class where students lead their own grounding routines, selecting from a menu of Root strategies (garden walk, soil observation, breathing exercise). Includes student interviews about why Root matters.
- **Target Audience:** 9-12 educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Observe student-directed grounding; hear student perspectives on the Root phase; note teacher facilitation of choice.

### VL-1.6: Regulate Phase -- Co-Regulation Strategies
- **Duration:** 16 minutes
- **Description:** Demonstrations of co-regulation strategies across grade bands, including parallel breathing, calm corner use, movement breaks, and sensory tools. Emphasizes the co-regulation continuum from adult-led to student-directed.
- **Target Audience:** All educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Demonstrate at least three co-regulation strategies; explain the co-regulation to self-regulation continuum; identify when to transition from Regulate to Reflect.

### VL-1.7: Reflect Phase -- Facilitating Deep Thinking
- **Duration:** 20 minutes
- **Description:** Extended classroom footage showing the Reflect phase in a 4th-grade math lesson using garden measurement data. Includes TRACE protocol integration and examples of all seven reasoning moves.
- **Target Audience:** All educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Observe TRACE facilitation within the Reflect phase; identify reasoning moves in student discourse; note teacher questioning techniques.

### VL-1.8: Restore Phase -- Reframing Errors
- **Duration:** 15 minutes
- **Description:** Two classroom examples of the Restore phase in action: one in a 2nd-grade reading lesson and one in a 7th-grade math lesson. Shows how teachers normalize errors, facilitate error analysis, and support students in returning to Reflect.
- **Target Audience:** All educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Observe trauma-informed error response; see the Restore-to-Reflect transition; hear students articulate growth mindset language.

### VL-1.9: Reconnect Phase -- Application and Integration
- **Duration:** 17 minutes
- **Description:** Examples of the Reconnect phase across multiple content areas, showing how students apply learning to real-world contexts, garden applications, and community connections. Includes portfolio documentation and peer sharing.
- **Target Audience:** All educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Observe transfer and application strategies; see portfolio integration; note community connection activities.

### VL-1.10: Full 5Rs Lesson Cycle (Uninterrupted)
- **Duration:** 45 minutes
- **Description:** A complete, unedited 5Rs lesson cycle in a 5th-grade classroom, from Root to Reconnect. Includes a post-lesson teacher reflection interview. Designed for in-depth analysis during PD sessions.
- **Target Audience:** All educators (Year 1-2)
- **Learning Objectives:** Observe a full implementation cycle; identify phase transitions; analyze teacher decision-making; note student regulation patterns.

## Category 2: TRACE Protocol Examples

### VL-2.1: TRACE Protocol Overview
- **Duration:** 14 minutes
- **Description:** An animated explainer introducing the five TRACE moves (Think, Reason, Articulate, Check, Extend) with classroom examples of each. Includes a reference chart of questioning stems.
- **Target Audience:** All educators (Year 1)
- **Learning Objectives:** Name and describe all five TRACE moves; identify questioning stems for each move; understand the relationship between TRACE and reasoning moves.

### VL-2.2: TRACE in Mathematics (K-2)
- **Duration:** 12 minutes
- **Description:** A first-grade teacher uses TRACE to guide students through a garden-based counting and sorting activity. Emphasis on developmentally appropriate Think and Articulate moves.
- **Target Audience:** K-2 educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Observe age-appropriate TRACE facilitation; see scaffolding for young learners; note garden-math integration.

### VL-2.3: TRACE in Mathematics (3-5)
- **Duration:** 16 minutes
- **Description:** A fourth-grade teacher facilitates a full TRACE sequence during a lesson on area and perimeter using raised bed garden design. Students measure, calculate, and present reasoning.
- **Target Audience:** 3-5 educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Observe full TRACE sequence in math; see reasoning moves (Decompose, Verify); note garden-standards alignment.

### VL-2.4: TRACE in Mathematics (6-8)
- **Duration:** 18 minutes
- **Description:** An eighth-grade algebra class uses TRACE to analyze growth rate data from the school garden. Students develop linear equations and justify their reasoning to peers.
- **Target Audience:** 6-8 educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Observe advanced TRACE facilitation; see reasoning moves (Generalize, Transform); note data-driven instruction.

### VL-2.5: TRACE in Science (3-5)
- **Duration:** 15 minutes
- **Description:** A third-grade science class uses TRACE to investigate seed germination conditions. Students design experiments, articulate hypotheses, and check results against predictions.
- **Target Audience:** 3-5 educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Observe TRACE in scientific inquiry; see reasoning moves (Question, Verify); note NGSS alignment.

### VL-2.6: TRACE in Science (6-8)
- **Duration:** 17 minutes
- **Description:** A seventh-grade life science class uses TRACE to analyze soil composition data from the school garden. Students use microscopes, collect data, and extend findings to ecosystem health.
- **Target Audience:** 6-8 educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Observe TRACE in laboratory settings; see data analysis and Extend move; note cross-curricular connections.

### VL-2.7: TRACE in ELA (K-2)
- **Duration:** 11 minutes
- **Description:** A second-grade teacher uses TRACE during a read-aloud of a garden-themed picture book. Students think about characters, reason about motivations, and articulate connections to their own garden experiences.
- **Target Audience:** K-2 educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Observe TRACE in literacy instruction; see early comprehension strategy development; note garden-text connections.

### VL-2.8: TRACE in ELA (6-8)
- **Duration:** 16 minutes
- **Description:** An eighth-grade English class uses TRACE to analyze a nonfiction text about food justice. Students engage in evidence-based argumentation, check sources, and extend to community action proposals.
- **Target Audience:** 6-8 educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Observe TRACE in critical literacy; see reasoning moves (Compare, Connect); note culturally responsive text selection.

### VL-2.9: The Seven Reasoning Moves -- Classroom Examples
- **Duration:** 22 minutes
- **Description:** A compilation of classroom clips illustrating each of the seven reasoning moves (Decompose, Compare, Question, Verify, Generalize, Transform, Connect) across grade bands and content areas.
- **Target Audience:** All educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Identify each reasoning move in context; hear student language associated with each move; note teacher prompts that elicit reasoning.

### VL-3.1: Starting a School Garden -- Planning and Design
- **Duration:** 24 minutes
- **Description:** Step-by-step guide to planning a school garden, including site selection, design considerations, raised bed construction, soil preparation, and budgeting. Filmed at three RootWork partner schools.

- **Learning Objectives:** Identify key planning steps; understand site requirements; develop a realistic garden budget.

### VL-3.2: Seasonal Garden Calendar for Georgia

- **Target Audience:** All educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Plan garden activities by season; align planting with the school year; identify winter garden strategies.

### VL-3.5: Garden-Based Math Activities by Grade Band

### VL-3.6: Garden-Based Science Activities by Grade Band

### VL-3.7: Garden-Based Literacy Activities
- **Duration:** 16 minutes

- **Target Audience:** ELA educators across grade bands

## Category 4: Regulation Strategy Demonstrations

### VL-4.1: Sensory Grounding with Garden Elements
- **Duration:** 12 minutes
- **Description:** Demonstrations of five sensory grounding strategies using garden materials: smelling herbs, touching soil and seeds, listening to nature sounds, observing plant details, and tasting garden produce.
- **Target Audience:** All educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Demonstrate five sensory grounding strategies; explain the neuroscience of sensory regulation; adapt strategies for classroom and garden settings.

### VL-4.2: Breathing Techniques for Regulation
- **Duration:** 10 minutes
- **Description:** Step-by-step demonstrations of six breathing techniques appropriate for school settings: belly breathing, square breathing, flower breathing (K-2), roots and branches breathing, 4-7-8 breathing, and coherent breathing.
- **Target Audience:** All educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Demonstrate six breathing techniques; identify age-appropriate techniques by grade band; integrate breathing into daily routines.

### VL-4.3: Movement-Based Regulation Strategies
- **Duration:** 14 minutes
- **Description:** Demonstrations of movement strategies that support regulation: garden stretches, walking meditation through garden paths, heavy work (carrying soil, watering), and rhythmic movement activities.
- **Target Audience:** All educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Demonstrate four movement-based regulation strategies; explain why movement supports regulation; identify opportunities for movement in the school day.

### VL-4.4: Co-Regulation in Action -- Teacher Demonstrations
- **Duration:** 18 minutes
- **Description:** Real classroom footage of teachers using co-regulation strategies with dysregulated students. Includes narrated decision-making and post-interaction reflections. Identities are protected.
- **Target Audience:** All educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Observe effective co-regulation; identify key co-regulation behaviors (proximity, tone, pacing); distinguish co-regulation from compliance-based responses.

### VL-4.5: Building a Classroom Regulation Toolkit
- **Duration:** 11 minutes
- **Description:** A walkthrough of how to create and introduce a classroom regulation toolkit, including physical materials, visual supports, choice boards, and garden-based regulation options.
- **Target Audience:** All educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Design a classroom regulation toolkit; introduce regulation tools to students; maintain and update the toolkit across the year.

### VL-4.6: Educator Self-Regulation Practices
- **Duration:** 15 minutes
- **Description:** Educators share their personal regulation practices and how self-regulation supports their ability to be trauma-informed practitioners. Includes guided practices the viewer can try.
- **Target Audience:** All educators
- **Learning Objectives:** Identify personal regulation strategies; develop a daily regulation routine; understand the connection between educator regulation and student well-being.

## Accessing the Video Library

All videos are available through the RootGuide platform under the **Professional Development > Video Library** tab. Educators can:

- Search by category, grade band, or competency domain
- Create custom playlists for PLC or self-directed learning
- Download accompanying reflection guides and handouts
- Track viewing history for PD credit documentation
- Rate and comment on videos to support community learning

Videos are also available for offline viewing through the RootGuide mobile app (iOS and Android). New videos are added quarterly based on educator feedback and emerging needs.

## Video Production and Submission

Educators who wish to contribute to the Video Library may submit classroom footage or testimonials through the RootGuide platform. All submissions are reviewed by the RootWork PD team for quality, alignment, and privacy compliance before publication. Contributing to the Video Library counts toward renewal PD hours for credentialed educators.

# FG2G Video Library Index

## Overview

The FG2G Video Library provides visual models of effective implementation across all aspects of the curriculum. Videos serve as mirrors for educator reflection, windows into diverse implementation contexts, and models for skill development. Each video is tagged with metadata for easy searching and is accompanied by a viewing guide with reflection prompts.

> *"Reading about the 5Rs is essential. Watching them in action is transformative. The video library bridges theory and practice by showing what trauma-informed, garden-based learning looks like in real classrooms and real gardens."*

## Using the Video Library

### For Individual Study
1. Select videos aligned to your current PD module or growth area
2. Watch with the accompanying viewing guide
3. Complete reflection prompts in your professional growth journal
4. Discuss observations with your peer learning partner

### For PLC Sessions
1. Select 1-2 videos relevant to the PLC's current focus
2. Watch together, pausing for discussion at designated points
3. Use the observation protocol to structure conversation
4. Identify specific practices to try before the next meeting

### For Credentialing
- Videos serve as models for the teaching demonstrations required in Tiers 2 and 3
- Candidates may reference specific videos in their portfolio narratives
- Video analysis assignments are included in several PD modules

## Technical Specifications

- **Format:** MP4 (H.264) for download; streaming via RootGuide platform
- **Accessibility:** All videos include closed captions and audio descriptions
- **Languages:** English with Spanish subtitles available for Series 1 and 2
- **Access:** Available through the FG2G digital platform with educator credentials
- **Updates:** New videos added each academic year based on implementation sites
